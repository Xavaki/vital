    How time is counted

    Time counts only while a watchlisted site is the active tab in the focused Firefox window. Background tabs, other windows and time away from Firefox don't count — music playing in a background tab costs nothing.

    Every counted second comes off today's allowance, which resets at midnight. Running out blocks nothing; the toolbar badge and popup just show how far over you are.

    Privacy

    Everything stays on this device. Vital stores hostnames, visit times and per-day totals — never full URLs, page titles, queries or page content. No account, no network requests, no telemetry. Private-browsing windows are not tracked.
